How the flywheel works

  1. 1
    Builders launch ideasEvery cleared idea launches on ponsfamily.com through StartupX Launch.
  2. 2
    Their tokens tradeTrading on each launch generates creator fees.
  3. 3
    Fees go to buybacksThe 20% share from every launch, plus half of $STARTUP's own creator fees, buy back $STARTUP. The other half funds the platform's development.
  4. 4
    $STARTUP fills the vaultEvery time 1% of the supply has been bought back, it's locked on Streamflow until Sunday.
  5. 5
    The top 5 ideas get airdroppedEvery week, the five highest-ranked ideas receive $STARTUP from the vault.
