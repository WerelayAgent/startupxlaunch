Questions

Why launch through StartupX Launch instead of ponsfamily.com directly?The audit tells traders your idea is original and has something real behind it, and the locked fee split shows your funding can't be redirected.
Can the fee split be changed after launch?No. It's set and locked on-chain in the same step as the launch, and anyone can verify it.
Does StartupX Launch hold any tokens?No. The deployer makes no initial buy and holds 0% of every token it launches.
Is my identity shown anywhere?No. Audit cards show the idea and the verdict only.
Who pays for the DEX Screener profile?The builder does, from their own wallet, right after launch. It's a public sign of commitment, and it's required for the weekly airdrop.
Where do $STARTUPX's own fees go?For now, half of each claim buys back $STARTUPX and half funds the platform's development through a public treasury wallet. The buyback share will increase as the project becomes established. The 20% share from other launches goes entirely to buybacks.
What happens to the bought-back $STARTUPX?Every time 1% of the supply has been bought back, that 1% is locked on Streamflow until Sunday 20:00 UTC. All of the week's locks unlock together, and the five highest-ranked ideas by market cap each receive an equal share.

Security: what your wallet signs

StartupX Launch never asks for your recovery phrase, never requests token approvals or spending permissions, and never moves anything from your wallet beyond the costs listed below. Because the site is new, wallet security tools such as Phantom and Pocket Universe may show a warning until they've reviewed it. These are the only things you're ever asked to sign:

  1. 1
    Sign inA standard Sign In With Robinhood Chain message for this site. It proves you own the wallet; it's not a transaction and costs nothing.
  2. 2
    Launch, approval 1 of 2: create the tokenCalls ponsfamily.com's own program to create your token. Your wallet pays the launch cost, about 0.02 ETH. The official StartupX Launch deployer is named as creator and makes no purchase.
  3. 3
    Launch, approval 2 of 2: lock the fee splitCalls ponsfamily.com's fee program to set the creator-fee split: 80% to your wallet, 20% to the $STARTUPX buyback wallet. ponsfamily.com allows this once, so it's locked for good.
  4. 4
    Claim feesCalls ponsfamily.com's fee program to pay out your token's fee vault: 80% to you, 20% to the buyback wallet. You pay only the network fee.
