Start your application

Connect and sign in with the wallet that should receive your 80% of the fees. It can't be changed after launch.

Use a fresh wallet for your launch

We recommend a new wallet created just for this idea, holding only enough ETH for the launch cost (about 0.02 ETH plus network fees). It's the wallet your 80% of the fees is paid to, so keep its recovery phrase safe. StartupX Launch never asks for your recovery phrase, never requests token approvals, and never moves anything from your wallet beyond the launch cost you approve.

Your wallet may show a warning because StartupX Launch is a new site. See exactly what each step does.

What the audit checks

Original on StartupX LaunchThe idea hasn't launched here before. Names and tickers can repeat; the idea can't.
Clear and interestingA real problem, a specific user and a product with its own angle.
Something behind itAt least one of: something built, an audience, relevant experience, or a concrete plan for the fees.

If it's cleared

  • Your token launches on ponsfamily.com with an address ending in idea. You cover the launch cost, about 0.02 ETH.
  • The fee split is locked on-chain at launch: 80% to you, claimable on ponsfamily.com at any time, and 20% to the $STARTUPX buyback.
  • You pay for your token's DEX Screener profile from your own wallet, as a public sign of commitment.
  • Your idea gets a public audit card and can be picked for the weekly top 5 $STARTUPX airdrop. Your identity is never shown.

If it isn't cleared, you can submit a new or improved idea after 7 days.
